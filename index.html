The recorded instrument

PATENT RECORD VOL. 2114

THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, to all to whom these presents shall come, Greeting:

have given and granted, and by these presents do give and grant, unto the said Elias Harper, and to his heirs, the North East quarter of Section Twelve, containing one hundred and sixty acres.

Given under my hand, at the City of Washington, the fourteenth day of March, 1838.

Received for record April 2, 1838. Recorded in Patent Record Vol. 2, Page 114. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 1 points to Patent Vol. 2, Pg. 114: the patent out of the United States, where the chain begins.

DEED RECORD VOL. 31402

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS, that Elias Harper and wife, in consideration of the sum to them paid by Jacob Harper,

do hereby grant, bargain, sell and convey unto the said Jacob Harper, his heirs and assigns forever, eighty (80) acres, more or less, in the Northeast Quarter of Section Twelve.

and will warrant and defend the same against all lawful claims whatsoever.

Received for record September 27, 1871, at 11 o'clock A.M. Recorded in Deed Record Vol. 31, Page 402. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 2 points to Deed Vol. 31, Pg. 402: the deed that carved the 80 acres out of the quarter section.

DEED RECORD VOL. 98215

being eighty (80) acres, more or less, in the Northeast Quarter of Section Twelve;

EXCEPTING AND RESERVING unto the Grantor, his heirs and assigns, an undivided one-half (1/2) of all the oil and gas in and under said premises.

TO HAVE AND TO HOLD said premises unto the said Grantee, his heirs and assigns forever.

Received for record May 14, 1921, at 10:20 A.M. Recorded in Deed Record Vol. 98, Page 215. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 3 points to Deed Vol. 98, Pg. 215. Open it and you are reading the 1921 deed itself.

DEED RECORD VOL. 11266

CERTIFICATE OF TRANSFER. Probate Court, Sample County. Estate of Jacob Harper, deceased.

The undivided one-half (1/2) interest in the oil and gas reserved in Deed Record Vol. 98, Page 215, passed by inheritance to Ruth Harper Keller and Thomas J. Harper, in equal shares.

Witness my signature and the seal of said Court, February 3, 1931.

Received for record February 10, 1931, at 2:05 P.M. Recorded in Deed Record Vol. 112, Page 66. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 4 points to Deed Vol. 112, Pg. 66: the certificate that passed the reserved half to two heirs.

LEASE RECORD VOL. 22310

OIL AND GAS LEASE, made August 16, 1951, by Samuel T. Dawson and wife, Lessors, to Sample Oil Company, Lessee.

This lease shall remain in force for a term of ten (10) years from this date, and as long thereafter as oil or gas is produced from said land by the Lessee.

Said land being eighty (80) acres, more or less, in the Northeast Quarter of Section Twelve.

Received for record September 4, 1951, at 1:15 P.M. Recorded in Lease Record Vol. 22, Page 310. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 5 points to Lease Vol. 22, Pg. 310: the 1951 lease and its ten-year primary term.

LEASE RECORD VOL. 3912

RELEASE OF OIL AND GAS LEASE. Sample Oil Company, Lessee, for value received,

does hereby release, surrender and cancel the oil and gas lease dated August 16, 1951, recorded in Lease Record Vol. 22, Page 310.

In witness whereof, Sample Oil Company has signed this release October 5, 1962.

Received for record October 22, 1962, at 3:30 P.M. Recorded in Lease Record Vol. 39, Page 12. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 6 points to Lease Vol. 39, Pg. 12: the release that clears the 1951 lease from the record.

DEED RECORD VOL. 18777

being the same premises conveyed to the Grantors by deed recorded in Deed Record Vol. 98, Page 215;

SUBJECT TO the exception and reservation of one-half (1/2) of the oil and gas contained in said deed.

TO HAVE AND TO HOLD said premises unto the said Grantees, their heirs and assigns forever.

Received for record June 2, 1966, at 9:40 A.M. Recorded in Deed Record Vol. 187, Page 77. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 7 points to Deed Vol. 187, Pg. 77: the 1966 deed, which carries the reservation forward.

MORTGAGE RECORD VOL. 74141

MORTGAGE. Harlan E. Whitfield and wife, the Mortgagors, to secure payment of their promissory note of even date, grant with mortgage covenants to Sample County Savings Bank the premises conveyed to them this day.

Margin: Satisfied and released of record, March 14, 1986. See Mortgage Record Vol. 131, Page 9.

Received for record June 2, 1966, at 9:45 A.M. Recorded in Mortgage Record Vol. 74, Page 141. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 8 points to Mortgage Vol. 74, Pg. 141: the 1966 mortgage, with its release noted in the margin.

OFFICIAL RECORD VOL. 4121184

Mary L. Whitfield, widow and surviving spouse of Harlan E. Whitfield, deceased, the Grantor, for valuable consideration paid,

grants, with general warranty covenants, to Miller Ridge Farms LLC, the Grantee, eighty (80) acres, more or less, in the Northeast Quarter of Section Twelve.

Prior instrument reference: Deed Record Vol. 187, Page 77.

Received for record July 15, 2009, at 10:02 A.M. Recorded in Official Record Vol. 412, Page 1184. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 9 points to Official Record Vol. 412, Pg. 1184: the 2009 deed into the current owner of record.

How it works

The software does the searching.
An examiner does the judging.

The software

  • Pulls the county records
  • Builds the chain of title
  • Prepares the run sheet, reports, maps and plots
  • Cites the recorded instrument for each entry

The examiner

  • Reviews the chain against the instruments
  • Applies professional judgment where the record is unclear
  • Signs off before the work is delivered
