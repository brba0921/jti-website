The product

From tax records to the completed abstract.

Eight deliverables, in the order JTI produces them. Each is built from the county record and cites the instrument it came from, so anyone on your team can check it against the record.

Tax Records

The parcel as the county assesses it.

Owner, legal description, valuation and tax status, from the county auditor.

Mapping

The tract, located on the ground.

The parcel placed in its section, township and range, with historical maps added as the record requires.

Chain of Title

Every conveyance, patent to present.

Surface and minerals traced separately, including the 1921 severance and the heirs who took the reserved half.

Runsheet

Every entry cites the recorded instrument.

Select any entry to open the recorded instrument behind it.

PATENT RECORD VOL. 2114

THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, to all to whom these presents shall come, Greeting:

have given and granted, and by these presents do give and grant, unto the said Elias Harper, and to his heirs, the North East quarter of Section Twelve, in Township Thirteen North, of Range Six West, containing one hundred and sixty acres.

Given under my hand, at the City of Washington, the fourteenth day of March, 1838.

Received for record May 18, 1841. Recorded in Patent Record Vol. 2, Page 114. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 1 points to Patent Vol. 2, Pg. 114: the patent out of the United States, where the chain begins.

DEED RECORD VOL. 31402

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS, that Elias Harper and wife, in consideration of the sum to them paid by Jacob Harper,

do hereby grant, bargain, sell and convey unto the said Jacob Harper, his heirs and assigns forever, eighty (80) acres in the Northeast Quarter of Section Twelve, Township Thirteen North, Range Six West, bounded and described as follows: Beginning at the southwest corner of said Quarter Section; thence by five courses and distances, in chains, … to the place of beginning.

and will warrant and defend the same against all lawful claims whatsoever.

Received for record September 27, 1871, at 11 o'clock A.M. Recorded in Deed Record Vol. 31, Page 402. Recorder, Sample County.

Entry 2 points to Deed Vol. 31, Pg. 402: the deed that carved the 80 acres out of the quarter section.

DEED RECORD VOL. 98215

being eighty (80) acres in the Northeast Quarter of Section Twelve, Township Thirteen North, Range Six West, described as follows: Beginning at the southwest corner of said Quarter Section; thence N 89¼° E 39.22 chains; thence N 1¾° W 13.60 chains; thence N 41¼° W 9.90 chains; thence S 89¼° W 32.56 chains; thence S ¾° E 21.08 chains to the place of beginning, containing eighty (80) acres;

How it works

The software does the searching.
An examiner does the judging.

The software

  • Pulls the county records
  • Builds the chain of title
  • Prepares the runsheet, reports, maps and plots
  • Cites the recorded instrument for each entry

The examiner

  • Reviews the chain against the instruments
  • Applies professional judgment where the record is unclear
  • Signs off before the work is delivered
